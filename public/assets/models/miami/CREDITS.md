# Miami Beach assets

## convertible.glb

Ocean Drive's hero convertible, taken from
[StarKnightt/ocean-drive](https://github.com/StarKnightt/ocean-drive)
(MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Prasenjit).

Local changes: the `convertible_L1` LOD node is stripped at load, the body paint
material is tinted per placed car, and the cars use a cheap blob shadow instead of
the full shadow pass.
